## Interview questions (3)
1. Fixed outcome-based fee vs billing time on an AI project: what do you need to know first? Pattern: pricing/profitability case; JD asks for commercial judgment on AI offerings. Basis: facts 5, 6; `research-consulting-ai.md` section 3. Strong answer: a measurable outcome, who controls the lever, baseline, risk split, what the client must supply.
2. "Juniors are obsolete." Agree? Pattern: structured "should we" opinion question. Basis: facts 8 and 9 conflict. Strong answer states both sides and the data that would settle it (intake by firm, utilisation, project margins).
3. How do you know whether an AI answer is inside or outside the tool's frontier? Pattern: "judge the output" probe in AI-assisted rounds; the McKinsey Lilli pilot (FT, 14 Jan 2026) tests whether candidates "challenge it, put it into context". Strong answer: check one number against an independent source, test on a known-answer case, ask for the assumptions.

## Indian business example
**Illustrative:** a Mumbai mid-size consulting firm with 200 analysts pitches a retail bank on an AI-assisted credit-policy review and puts 30% of its fee at risk against a target cut in loan-approval turnaround. The student must say which outcome is measurable, who controls the lever (the bank's branch staff), and how the firm could lose money even if the AI works. No real firm, no real numbers. Real India evidence is thin: Deloitte India COO Nitin Kini (TelecomTalk, 30 Mar 2026) spoke of training ~30,000 staff and hiring "similar profiles"; the "50,000 hires" is the interviewer's number. ~ not re-opened today, so not used as a fact.

## Misconceptions
1. "AI will wipe out entry-level consulting jobs." The evidence is mixed: UK Big Four intake down; BCG and Bain say flat or up. PwC UK's own explanation for its cut was mostly "the wider slowdown in investment" (Fortune, 8 Sep 2025; ~ not re-opened).
2. "AI makes every task better." The outside-frontier task is the counterexample: 19 percentage points less likely to be correct.
3. "Consulting is moving to outcome pricing." McKinsey about a quarter, BCG under a third overall; most fees are still not outcome-based.
**Stat block:** 19 percentage points less likely correct outside the frontier, set against +32% quality inside it (both Organization Science 2026).
