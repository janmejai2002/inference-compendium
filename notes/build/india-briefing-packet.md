## Facts
1. **DPDP Rules notified 14 Nov 2025; Act enacted 11 Aug 2023.** PIB: "The Government of India has notified the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Rules, 2025, marking the full operationalisation of the DPDP Act, 2023"; Act "Enacted by Parliament on 11 August 2023". Source: PIB/MeitY, "Government notifies DPDP Rules to empower citizens and protect privacy", 14 Nov 2025. https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2190014&reg=3&lang=2 . A. ✓ opened 2026-11-01
2. **18-month phased compliance.** Same release: "an 18-month phased compliance timeline". Fiduciaries must answer access/correction/update/erasure requests "within a maximum of 90 days". ✓ opened 2026-11-01 (the 12-month consent-manager step and the 13 vs 14 Nov gazette date appear only in law-firm coverage; not used).
3. **DPDP duties that touch AI work:** standalone plain-language consent notices stating the specific purpose; verifiable consent for children's data; Significant Data Fiduciaries get "independent audits, impact assessments" and possible localisation; appeals against the Data Protection Board go to TDSAT. Same source. ✓ opened 2026-11-01
4. **RBI FREE-AI report, 13 Aug 2025: 7 Sutras, 26 recommendations, six pillars.** RBI press release 2025-2026/902: "The Committee has developed 7 Sutras to serve as the foundational principles for AI adoption." Source: RBI, "Report of the Committee ... (FREE-AI) in the Financial Sector", 13 Aug 2025. https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=61018 . A. ✓ opened 2026-11-01 (page text confirmed 7 sutras and 26 recommendations; pillar names not shown on the page, so do not name them).
5. **The seven sutras (national guidelines adopt them):** Trust is the Foundation; People First; Innovation over Restraint; Fairness & Equity; Accountability; Understandable by Design; Safety, Resilience & Sustainability. Source: MeitY, India AI Governance Guidelines (Nov 2025), Part 1: "Seven guiding principles or sutras have been adapted from the RBI's FREE-AI Committee report". https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc2025115685601.pdf . A. ✓ opened 2026-11-01
6. **No separate AI law "at this stage".** Guidelines, Part 2: "at this stage, a separate law to regulate AI is not needed given the current assessment of risks. However, timely and consistent enforcement of applicable laws is required to build trust and mitigate harm." Existing IT, data protection, consumer protection and criminal law apply. Six pillars: Infrastructure, Capacity Building, Policy & Regulation, Risk Mitigation, Accountability, Institutions. Same source. ✓ opened 2026-11-01
7. **IndiaAI Mission outlay Rs 10,371.92 crore, Cabinet approval 7 Mar 2024.** "comprehensive national-level IndiaAI mission with a budget outlay of Rs.10,371.92 crore"; compute of "10,000 or more" GPUs via public-private partnership; seven components (Compute, Innovation Centre for indigenous foundation models, Datasets, Application Development, FutureSkills, Startup Financing, Safe & Trusted AI). https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2012355 . A. ✓ opened 2026-11-01
8. **Compute as of 31 Aug 2025: 38,231 GPUs.** Guidelines: "Over 38,231 GPUs are being made available to startups, researchers and developers at subsidised rates"; AIKosh "1,500 datasets and 217 AI models"; four startups in phase 1 of sovereign models, "up to 25% of compute costs", grants 40% / equity 60%. Same source as 5. ✓ opened 2026-11-01
9. **India AI Impact Summit, Feb 2026.** PIB 2 Mar 2026: held "16-21 February 2026"; declaration "endorsed by 92 countries and international organisations"; "13" frontier developers signed the New Delhi Frontier AI Impact Commitments; "38,000+ GPUs already provisioned ... an additional 20,000 GPUs will be added"; "Over USD 200 billion in AI-related investments are expected" (an expectation, not a result). https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2234343&reg=3&lang=1 . A. ✓ opened 2026-11-01
10. **India on Claude: 5.8% of Claude.ai use, 2nd to the US; 45.2% of tasks software-related, 1st globally; 101st of 116 on working-age per-capita use.** Anthropic, "India Country Brief: The Anthropic Economic Index", Ruth Appel, 16 Feb 2026; Claude.ai conversations 13-20 Nov 2025, India n=58,098 of 975,160. Vietnam 42.1%, Egypt 39.2%. Top states: Maharashtra 15.5%, Tamil Nadu 13.2%, Karnataka 12.7%, Delhi 10.5%. Print the caveat: one lab's consumer product only. https://www.anthropic.com/research/india-brief-economic-index . A. ✓ opened 2026-11-01

## Indian business example
Illustrative: a mid-size NBFC pilots a chatbot that reads loan documents. Under DPDP it needs a standalone consent notice naming the purpose; following FREE-AI's accountability sutra it names an owner for model errors and keeps a log. No real firm, no numbers.

## Misconceptions
1. "India has no rules for AI." No AI-specific statute, but DPDP, IT, consumer and sector rules already apply (facts 1-3, 6).
2. "FREE-AI binds every company." It is a committee report for finance with recommendations; the national Guidelines are voluntary. Do not call it law (see Gaps).
3. "Most use means most intensive use." India is 2nd by volume share but 101st of 116 per working-age head (fact 10).

## Stat block
"45.2%": share of India's Claude.ai tasks that are software-related, highest of any country (Anthropic, Feb 2026).
