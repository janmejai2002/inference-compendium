  * *Evidence:* Near-universal curriculum topic (9 of 12) in [research-canon.md](file:///C:/Users/Janmejai/compendium/notes/toc/research-canon.md). * **Change Management, Workplace Adoption, and Organizational Re-engineering**

  * *Evidence Gap:* [research-canon.md](file:///C:/Users/Janmejai/compendium/notes/toc/research-canon.md) shows the AI stack is absent across almost all management curricula (0–1 of 12). Strategy courses outnumber implementation/stack courses 9.5 to 1.
  * *Assessment:* While relevant as industry background for consulting tech practices, treating cloud compute and foundry economics as a mandatory standalone chapter for non-technical managers is unsupported by general management syllabi and job asks.

* **Chapter 11: Universal "Pyramid Collapse" and Wholesale Junior Hiring Elimination**
  * *Evidence Gap:* [research-consulting-ai.md](file:///C:/Users/Janmejai/compendium/notes/toc/research-consulting-ai.md) proves junior intake trends are split: Big Four UK cuts are offset by steady or expanding MBB hiring (Bain +25%, BCG flat, McKinsey NA +12%). McKinsey State of AI 2026 finds overall job losses fall far short of past forecasts.
  * *Assessment:* Framing Chapter 11 around the imminent destruction of junior white-collar jobs overstates the empirical evidence and conflates tech-sector developer trends with business management intake.

* **Chapter 3: Standalone "Tokenomics" and Deep Inference Cost Optimization**
  * *Evidence Gap:* [research-canon.md](file:///C:/Users/Janmejai/compendium/notes/toc/research-canon.md) shows token pricing is taught in only 2 of 12 curricula (Yale and Karpathy). In general management and consulting JDs, token math appears in less than 1% of postings.
  * *Assessment:* Understanding that inference has a variable cost is crucial for consulting ROI cases, but dedicating an entire technical chapter to token pricing, context budgeting, and latency trade-offs is excessive for non-technical readers.

* **Chapters 12 & 13: Indian Campus Placement AI Interview Protocols**
  * *Evidence Gap:* [research-market-2026-10.md](file:///C:/Users/Janmejai/compendium/notes/toc/research-market-2026-10.md), [research-consulting-ai.md](file:///C:/Users/Janmejai/compendium/notes/toc/research-consulting-ai.md), and [DISCUSSION.md](file:///C:/Users/Janmejai/compendium/notes/toc/DISCUSSION.md) document zero public records, casebooks, or interview reports of dedicated AI interview rounds at Indian B-schools (IIMs/ISB). McKinsey Lilli interview pilots are confirmed only for North America and select European offices.
  * *Assessment:* Framing interview prep around specialized AI testing rounds risks preparing students for an interview format that does not yet exist in the Indian campus circuit.
