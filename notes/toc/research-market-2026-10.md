## 2. Recruiters' own words

- [B] ✓ **McKinsey pilots an "AI interview" with Lilli** (Fortune, Jake Angelo, 14 Jan 2026). Final-round pilot for North American candidates: "prompt the AI, review its output, and apply judgment" (via CaseBasix). Sternfels (HBR IdeaCast): "There isn't truth in AI models; there isn't judgment. Humans need to impose those parameters." ~20,000 agents vs ~40,000 employees; "migrating away from… the fee-for-service model". FT reported focus on business-school students. https://fortune.com/2026/01/14/how-to-get-hired-at-mckinsey-ai-tools-liberal-arts-creativity
  → Strongest single signal for the book's thesis: **the test is now judgment over AI output**, not knowing about AI. Need the HBR IdeaCast episode as the primary for the quotes. India rollout unknown → ask user's seniors.
- [A] ✓ **BCG, AI at Work 2026: "AI Is Reshaping Jobs Faster Than Companies Are Reshaping Work"** (3 Jun 2026). 11,749 workers, 14 markets. 74% of frontline white-collar staff are regular AI users; 47% spend more time *managing and directing AI* than doing the task; 30% have agents in workflows (13% a year ago); 52% have "limited understanding of what agents are"; 65% of managers expect agents to take half their job in 3 years. India above global average (no figure in release). https://www.bcg.com/press/3june2026-ai-reshaping-jobs-faster-than-companies-reshaping-work · slides: https://web-assets.bcg.com/e7/c7/00d913744cccb1e4f65bbf54fe86/ai-at-work-slideshow-june-2026.pdf (not opened)
  → "Managing and directing AI" is a skill we can teach. "52% don't understand agents" is the gap an MBA student can close in a week.
- [B] ~ **McKinsey, The state of AI in 2026: On the road to ROI** (25 Aug 2026, per secondary): 89% use AI, 44% scaling, only 37% report EBIT impact (flat). Job losses below forecasts. Primary chart page timed out: https://www.mckinsey.com/featured-insights/charts/ai-job-losses-fall-short-of-forecasts — retry with browse.py.

- [A] ~ **RBI FREE-AI report** (13 Aug 2025). 7 "Sutras", 6 pillars, 26 recommendations; board-approved AI policies; explainable, auditable credit models; customers told when dealing with AI. https://website.rbi.org.in/ur/web/rbi/-/press-releases/report-of-the-committee-to-develop-a-framework-for-responsible-and-ethical-enablement-of-artificial-intelligence-free-ai-in-the-financial-sector (not opened)

- [A] ✓ **Anthropic, India Country Brief, Economic Index** (16 Feb 2026): India is 5.8% of Claude.ai use (#2 after US) but ~101st per capita; 45.2% of tasks software-related (highest share of any country); 51.3% work-related; tasks taking 3.8 h done in 14.8 min with AI. https://www.anthropic.com/research/india-brief-economic-index
  → Indian *business* use of AI is thin relative to software use: the gap the book fills.

**Implication for the book:** model names change monthly; the book must teach what doesn't (how models work, what agents are, where they fail, how to judge output) and push "this month's frontier" into a dated, replaceable box or web-only page.
