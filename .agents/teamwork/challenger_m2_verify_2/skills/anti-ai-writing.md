# Anti-AI Writing & Human Editorial Standard

Key rules:
1. Specifics kill slop.
2. Banned vocabulary: testament, serves as a stark reminder, crucial, pivotal, vital, key, instrumental role, marks a shift, marks a pivotal turning point, sets the stage for, paves the way for, shapes the evolving landscape, rapidly shifting landscape, leaves an indelible mark, deeply rooted in, a testament to the enduring power of, underscores the critical importance of, delve / delving into.
3. Banned trailing participial clause: [Statement of fact], [highlighting / underscoring / reflecting / fostering / ensuring / encompassing / cultivating / offering valuable insights into X].
4. Banned attribution: featured in prominent media outlets, widely profiled in, written by a leading industry expert, garnered widespread attention, maintains an active presence in.
5. Banned melodrama & dichotomy: In the high-stakes world of, It is not a question of if, but when, It is not simply X; it is Y, Consider the case of, The lesson is unequivocal / the reality is sobering, At the end of the day, A double-edged sword / a double-edged reality.
6. Cut adverbs: devastatingly, critically, exponentially, fundamentally, catastrophically.
