# Anti-AI Writing Standard Local Summary

- Specifics kill slop. Replace general abstractions with verifiable proper nouns, exact dollar amounts, dates, named institutions, and regulatory statutes.
- Strictly banned words/phrases: "testament", "pivotal", "evolving landscape", "indelible", "delve", "crucial", "vital", "beacon", "in the high-stakes world of", "at the end of the day", "double-edged sword", "it is not a question of if, but when".
- Banned syntax: Trailing participial clauses like ", highlighting...", ", underscoring...", ", ensuring...", ", reflecting...".
- Human cadence: High burstiness (short blunt sentences alternating with rhythmic complex sentences).
- Active verbs of motion: route, dump, skip, strike, enforce, collapse, reject.
- Five-step audit: No "-ing" sentence tails, zero banned words, high sentence length variance, empirical grounding, authoritative unhurried tone.
